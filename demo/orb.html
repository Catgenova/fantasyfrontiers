HP orb — placeholder liquid, glass on top

Liquid is the delivered UI_orb_red.png clipped to a moving surface; the glass cover sits over it always, and the bezel laps its rim. Defaults are your stiffness 20 / damping 10 — which is overdamped, so the level glides without dipping past target. Drop damping under ~8.9 to get the overshoot back; the readout tells you which side of critical you are on.
How it moves. Fill level is a damped spring toward the target. Its velocity drives a tilt that tips the surface like liquid in a glass being carried, plus a slosh amplitude stacked on the idle ripple and decaying back to baseline. Both scale with the size of the HP change, so a chip ripples and a crit heaves.
A consequence of baked shading worth a decision: the red art has its own top-to-bottom gradient, and the liquid is clipped in place — so a nearly empty orb shows the amber bottom of that gradient rather than a small amount of the top red. That is physically right (the shading belongs to the vessel and its light, not to the liquid volume) but it does mean low HP reads warmer, not darker. The alternative is to squash the whole gradient into the fill, which keeps the colour constant but looks wrong as it moves.
Not simulated: the real spritesheet texture, and the low-HP treatment — the existing bar tints under 50% and 20%, so the orb probably wants to darken and pulse down there.
