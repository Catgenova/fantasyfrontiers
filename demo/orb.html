HP orb — placeholder liquid, glass on top

Fluid is painted from colours sampled out of UI_orb_red.png and clipped to a moving surface; the glass cover with its highlights sits still on top, and the bezel laps its rim. Defaults are your stiffness 20 / damping 10 — which is overdamped, so the level glides without dipping past target. Drop damping under ~8.9 to get the overshoot back; the readout tells you which side of critical you are on.
How it moves. Fill level is a damped spring toward the target. Its velocity drives a tilt that tips the surface like liquid in a glass being carried, plus a slosh amplitude stacked on the idle ripple and decaying back to baseline. Both scale with the size of the HP change, so a chip ripples and a crit heaves.
The colour comes from your reference, the gloss does not. UI_orb_red.png is sampled for its palette — crimson rgb(200,13,14), orange rgb(252,107,7), amber rgb(255,172,56) — and the fluid is painted from those. Drawing the sphere PNG itself is what dragged its highlights down with the level: those speculars and the rim light belong to the glass, which sits still on top. Now only the surface moves.
Low-HP darkening folds the ramp toward crimson as HP falls. Turn it off to see why it is there: a thin sliver is mostly its own bright surface band, so an almost-dead orb otherwise reads sunnier than a full one.
Not simulated: the real spritesheet texture, and the low-HP treatment — the existing bar tints under 50% and 20%, so the orb probably wants to darken and pulse down there.
